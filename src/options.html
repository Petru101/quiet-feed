Password

Off by default. Turn it on and changing your list, the site toggles or the on/off switch asks for a password first — in the popup and here. Unlocking lasts about five minutes.

Be clear-eyed about what this is: a speed bump against your own impulse to edit the list, not security. Anyone who can open about:debugging can read this extension's storage or just disable it. The password itself is never stored, only a salted hash of it, and there is no recovery — forget it and you'll have to reinstall the extension.

Backup and sync

Your list follows you to other devices signed in to the same Firefox account, and a copy is always kept on this machine as well. If a list ever grows past what Firefox Sync accepts, saving keeps working and says it kept the change locally instead.

Export writes your list and settings to a JSON file.
